# Market scan

1. Search for the latest close and recent move (1 day, 1 week) of:
   - Equity indices: S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, STOXX 600, DAX, FTSE 100, Nikkei 225, Hang Seng, MSCI Emerging Markets.
   - Rates: US 10-year Treasury yield, German 10-year Bund yield.
   - Volatility: VIX.
   - FX: EUR/USD, USD/JPY, DXY.
   - Commodities: Brent crude, gold, copper.
2. Search for the news of the last 3 days that explains the largest moves: central bank decisions and speeches, macro releases (inflation, jobs, PMI), earnings of index heavyweights, geopolitics.
3. Note the scheduled events of the coming week (central bank meetings, major data releases, large earnings).

Record each figure with its date and source. If two sources disagree, say so instead of picking one silently.
